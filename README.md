# The Moments We Miss

**Date:** 17 October 2019

---

We move through days without noticing,  
counting hours, chasing time,  
always looking toward tomorrow,  
believing the present will remain.

We laugh with people we love,  
never knowing which laugh will be the last.  
We say *“see you soon,”*  
without knowing what soon will mean.

The rain falls, the seasons change,  
familiar places slowly become distant.  
What once felt ordinary  
quietly becomes something we miss.

We never know the worth of a moment  
while we are standing inside it.  
We only understand its beauty  
when we can no longer return.

So hold the quiet mornings,  
the late-night conversations,  
the hands you can still reach,  
and the voices you can still hear.

Because life does not tell us  
which moments will matter most.

**You don't realize you're living a moment,  
until it becomes a memory.**
